"Imagine yourself exploring an abandoned bus station late at night. Flickering fluorescent lights cast pale, unsteady beams across cracked concrete floors littered with old newspapers and broken glass. The rusted steel frames of empty bus bays loom in the darkness, their destination signs frozen on routes long since cancelled. The air is heavy with the smell of damp concrete and diesel. Weeds push through gaps in the pavement, and peeling paint reveals layers of forgotten schedules on the walls. The distant hum of wind through broken windows is the only sound, punctuated by the occasional creak of a corrugated metal door swinging on its hinges. Shadows pool in the corners of the desolate waiting area, where rows of bolted-down plastic seats sit empty under a sagging ceiling. Where will you venture next in this forgotten place?"
